## Scenario A - customer reviews - PUBLIC DATA
| What | Source | Collected | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset: order reviews, orders, order items, products, category translation | Olist, https://github.com/olist/work-at-olist-data (datasets/), also published on Kaggle as "Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist" | 2026-11-14 | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (Kaggle release). Derived files in this repository (data/public/reviews_*.csv, data/eval/cx_eval_reviews.csv) are shared under the same licence, with attribution to Olist, for non-commercial use. |

Raw CSVs are not redistributed (size); `src/prepare_reviews.py` rebuilds everything from them. Olist had already anonymised
customers and replaced store names; we additionally mask e-mails, phone numbers, tax IDs, URLs and names after greetings.

## Scenario B - pricing pages - PUBLIC DATA
| What | Source | Collected | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 dated snapshots (2024-01 to 2026-11) of the public pricing pages of Notion, Slack, Asana, Trello, ClickUp, Zapier | Internet Archive Wayback Machine, raw mode (`id_`), list in [data/public/pricing_snapshots.csv](public/pricing_snapshots.csv) | 2026-11-14 | robots.txt of each vendor checked on 2026-11-14 (no rule on /pricing); about one request every two seconds; no login |

Raw pages are not redistributed (vendor copyright). The repository keeps the snapshot URLs, the reference labels (plan names and
prices, i.e. facts) and the extraction results. The vendors are examples of public pricing; they have no relation to this study.

## Scenario C - agency performance - SIMULATED
`src/generate_sim.py` (seed 42) creates daily data for 6 fictional creator brands and 5 traffic sources, May-October 2026,
with planted events logged in `data/simulated/events.csv`. Any resemblance of the fictional names to real brands is unintended.

## Tools
Python, pandas, DuckDB, Plotly, one LLM API (model and prices, checked 2026-11-14, in `workflows/common/llm.py`),
Microsoft Edge (headless) to print the PDF.
