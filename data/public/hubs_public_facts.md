## The problem Hubs says it solves (its own words, home page "Sound familiar?")
1. "My team uses AI every day. But our business doesn't get smarter from it."
2. "Our best people have figured out incredible AI workflows. Nobody else knows they exist."
3. "I don't need another chatbot. I need AI that knows our business."

SMB page adds six firm-level pains, e.g. "Everything we know is scattered and stuck in people's heads" and "We need solutions we can build without an IT team." (https://hubs.is/smb)

## The model: Build -> Spread -> Scale
- **Build**: "Someone sees an opportunity, they act on it."
- **Spread**: "When something works, it's reused."
- **Scale**: "When something's proven, it becomes a reusable agent."
- Vision page calls this the "intelligence fabric": Emerge -> Spread -> Evolve. (https://hubs.is/vision)

## Product surface (as described publicly)
- **Agentic Workspace**: "Real workflows. Built in minutes." Workflows "your team can deploy in one prompt". (https://hubs.is/)
- **Network**: what teammates and agents shared; "What's hot / happening / changed / next". (https://hubs.is/)
- **Spark**: the creation agent; "turns it into a structured plan", "Builds the full solution end-to-end". (https://hubs.is/product)
- **Building blocks**: Information, Intelligence ("Every leading AI model"), Automation, Action. (https://hubs.is/product)
- Agents "run on schedule, trigger on events, and deliver outputs automatically". (https://hubs.is/product)
- Example agents on the home page: **Proposal Gen, Risk Monitor, CX Pulse**, plus **Reports**. (https://hubs.is/)
- Workspace demo workflows: Risk Analysis, **Client Reporting**, Proposal Generator, **Competitor Watch**, Client Health, Deal Sourcing. (public site bundle behind https://hubs.is/)
- Eight "core patterns" (https://hubs.is/solutions): Revenue Analysis, **Competitor Watch** ("Track competitors for executive changes, pricing moves, or new offerings... you only hear about it when something actually shifts"), Meeting Rehearsal, Executive Briefing, **Proposal Generator**, Business Scenario, Expert Roundtable, Deal Sourcing.
- SMB examples include **Project Status Reports** ("Monday 8am, your client gets a polished update") and **Client Intelligence**. (https://hubs.is/smb)

## The role (job posting)
"Applied AI Product Associate, Business", remote (Europe & Americas), part-time or full-time.
Source: https://himalayas.app/companies/hubs-is/jobs/applied-ai-product-associate-business . The page is behind a bot check, so it was not re-fetched automatically; requirements are summarised from the posting text: own product feedback and implementation of solutions with real customers end to end, with the San Francisco team; meet customers live and build solutions; 1-2 years in solutions/sales engineering, business analysis, product (implementation or customer-facing) or BI/data platforms; good discovery and the right questions; comfortable moving fast with ambiguity; has used AI to solve business problems; fluent English, French/Spanish/Portuguese a strong plus.
